## HOW TO GET MORE PROJECTS (Veteran Consensus)

### What Actually Works:
1. **Complete qualifications** — every qual you pass opens new project families
2. **Submit quality work consistently** — "Submit quality work and the tasks will flow"
3. **Complete your profile** — fill in skills and background section (13 upvotes confirmed this helps)
4. **Vary the project families you work on** — don't just do one type
5. **Be patient** — veteran with 1.5 years had 0-5 projects, then suddenly got 30+ after proving quality
6. **Do quals you're confident in, skip ones you're not** — a failed qual is worse than no qual

### What Doesn't Work:
- ❌ There is NO secret hack or trick
- ❌ Refreshing constantly doesn't help
- ❌ Volume alone doesn't get you more projects — quality does
- ❌ Seniority alone doesn't guarantee work — performance matters

### The "Dash of Death" Warning:
- If you see "no projects available" message = could be temporary (came back after 10 days for one worker)
- It's NOT always permanent termination
- BUT it can be — workers call it the "Dash of Death" (DoD)
- Best hedge: sign up for competitor platforms (Outlier, Alignerr) as backup

## THE EASY VS HARD MONEY STRATEGY (143 upvotes, 71 comments)

### The Veteran Philosophy:
> "Give me 2 hours of $25/hour work I can burn through without breaking a sweat, rather than a $35/hour task I'm sick of after an hour" — Top 1% Commenter

### Strategy Tiers:

**🛋️ COUCH MODE ($20-25/hr)**
- Simple evaluation tasks, short instructions
- Good for: tired evenings, watching TV, decompressing
- Risk: lower pay but steady and stress-free
- Common format: rate responses, simple comparisons

**⚡ SWEET SPOT ($27-34/hr)**
- Best effort-to-pay ratio
- One worker: "making enough to pay my bills off 3 hours of work a day"
- The "$30-34/hr range" is cited as the ideal balance
- Some $30+ projects are EASIER than sub-$30 ones

**🧠 BRAIN BURNER ($35-50+/hr)**
- Complex instructions (often 20+ pages)
- Higher risk of escape hatching and losing work
- Best when: well-rested, focused, morning time
- Strategy: "begin and end with easy work, brain burners in the middle"

### The Mixed Strategy (Most Successful Workers):
1. Start the day with easy tasks to warm up
2. Do brain burners when energy is high
3. End with easy tasks to wind down
4. On Fridays / tired days → only easy projects
5. "Bounce back and forth between difficult and easy stuff"

### Key Insight from a Top Commenter:
> "Some of the higher paying $30+ ones are legitimately easier than the majority of under $30s. A lot of the more 'difficult' projects just look difficult on paper, and are much easier in practice."

## PROJECT TYPE BREAKDOWN (Community Intelligence)

### Easiest Project Types (Couch-Friendly):
- **Rate & Review** — compare two AI responses, pick the better one, write brief justification
- **Chat evaluation** — talk to a chatbot, rate its response
- **Image evaluation** — look at AI-generated images, rate quality
- **Simple math** — "weirdly easy $40/hr math-based ones, high school math at most" (rare but golden)

### Medium Difficulty:
- **Prompt creation** — write creative prompts for AI to answer
- **Fact-checking** — verify AI claims against real sources
- **Multi-turn conversation** — longer chat interactions with evaluation

### Hard But High Pay:
- **Coding evaluation** — review AI-generated code ($50-100/hr)
- **Research verification** — multi-pane data checking (like Barnard)
- **STEM tasks** — domain-specific expert work ($40+/hr)
- **Long-form writing** — extended response creation with strict rubrics

## TIME MANAGEMENT

### The 10-Hour Clock:
- Some projects give 10-hour timers
- No official pause — you work within the window
- Workers report using "exit work mode" and returning
- Strategy: break into manageable sessions

### Billing Best Practices:
- Use an external timer (phone stopwatch, Toggl, etc.)
- Bill accurately — inflated time = account flagged
- If a task breaks (submit error) → use escape hatch, still bill for time worked
- Report time promptly — red notification on Report Time tab is normal

### The Escape Hatch:
- Not a penalty — it's a feature for when things break
- You still get paid for time worked
- Better to escape hatch than submit bad work
- Don't fear it

## FEEDBACK SYSTEM

### How DA Evaluates You:
- Monthly (end-of-month?) automated feedback emails
- Ratings: Outstanding, Good, Needs Improvement
- Includes specific examples from your work
- Getting feedback = your account is being reviewed (GOOD sign)

### How to Get Outstanding:
1. Slow down — quality over speed
2. Read instructions thoroughly, every time
3. Be specific in comments — reference exact text
4. Follow formatting requirements exactly
5. Use Ctrl+F to search instructions for edge cases

## DROUGHT SURVIVAL GUIDE

### What Veterans Do During Droughts:
1. **Take the lower-paying projects** — "$20/hr projects you've been ignoring"
2. **Do every available qualification** — expands your project pool
3. **Sign up for competitor platforms** as backup (Outlier, Alignerr, Prolific)
4. **Don't panic** — droughts are cyclical, they always end
5. **Don't burn bridges** — keep quality high even on boring tasks

### Current Climate (April 2026):
- Platform-wide drought reported by many workers
- Some experienced workers dropped from 80+ to 16 projects in one afternoon
- Projects ARE cycling back — "certain hellenic puddle project has finally returned"
- New workers still getting onboarded and finding work
- End-of-month feedback cycle happening — good sign of active platform

## RED FLAGS & THINGS TO AVOID

1. ❌ **Never use AI to generate answers** — they check, and you'll be terminated
2. ❌ **Never pad hours** — automatic detection, account closure
3. ❌ **Never share project details publicly** — NDA violation
4. ❌ **Never submit work you're unsure about** — skip instead
5. ❌ **Never ignore updated instructions** — they change mid-project
6. ❌ **Don't treat $40+ projects as "easy money"** — they trap you if you can't finish
7. ❌ **Don't create multiple accounts** — permanent ban
