# The Human Voice: Style Guide & Vocabulary Blacklist
*A guide to stripping away AI patterns, tells, and structures to ensure absolute human authenticity.*

---

## 1. The Vocabulary Blacklist
AI models are trained to be polite, professional, and statistically average. Because of this, they rely on a recurring list of "buzzwords" that immediately trigger AI detectors and human suspicion. 

Never use these words in your rationales or prompts.

| Blacklisted Category | Words to Ban | Human Alternatives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-Risk "AI Tells"** | Delve, Tapestry, Landscape, Testament, Realm, Synergy, Beacon, Journey, Blueprint | Focus on, Look at, Picture, Area, Proof, Field, Plan, Path |
| **Robotic Transition Phrases** | Furthermore, In conclusion, It is important to note, At its core, To summarize, Indeed, Moreover | Also, So, Keep in mind, Basically, Ultimately, Truly |
| **Hype / Marketing Adjectives** | Seamless, Robust, Dynamic, Transformative, Cutting-edge, Innovative, Game-changer, Revolutionary | Smooth, Strong, Active, Major, New, Useful |
| **Cautious / Servile Fillers** | Generally speaking, It is worth mentioning, Personally, I believe, In my opinion (when obvious) | *State the opinion directly without the qualifying prefix.* |

## 2. Structural & Rhythm Patterns to Avoid
AI writes with a flat, predictable cadence. To write like a human, you must actively break the standard formatting habits of LLMs.

### The "Monotonous Cadence" Trap
*   **The AI Pattern:** AI writes sentences that are almost exactly the same length (usually 15-20 words). It flows perfectly, which makes it sound robotic.
*   **The Human Solution:** Vary sentence length dramatically. Write a long, detailed sentence. Follow it with a tiny, sharp one. Like this. It breaks the rhythm and sounds natural.

### The "Excessive Structure" Trap
*   **The AI Pattern:** AI loves bullet points, numbered lists, and rigid paragraphs that follow the strict "Topic Sentence $\rightarrow$ Example $\rightarrow$ Conclusion" format.
*   **The Human Solution:** Write in loose, conversational paragraphs. Avoid ending every paragraph with a neat summary sentence (e.g., *"Overall, this was a helpful interaction."*). Just stop writing when you've made your point.

### The "Over-Explanation" Trap
*   **The AI Pattern:** AI is overly cautious and explains *why* it is explaining things.
*   **The Human Solution:** Be direct. Do not write *"To explain this further, the reason is..."* Just state the reason.

## 3. The Human Writing Code
To ensure your writing is 100% human-authentic, apply these rules:

1.  **Use Contractions Naturally:** Write *don't*, *it's*, *can't*, and *won't*. AI often defaults to *do not*, *it is*, and *cannot* to maintain a formal tone.
2.  **Speak First, Write Second:** Read your rationale out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a coworker or a friend in a casual conversation, delete it.
3.  **Lead with Specificity:** AI uses vague generalizations (*"The user context was handled well"*). Humans use concrete names and details (*"Surfacing Rachel's birthday out of nowhere was creepy"*).
4.  **Embrace Mild Imperfections:** Humans write with sentence fragments, dashes, and informal punctuation. Don't worry about making your grammar textbook-perfect.
5.  **State Claims Directly:** Instead of writing *"The model's response was somewhat unhelpful because..."*, write *"The model completely ignored the main request and focused on the wrong thing."*
